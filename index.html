Hi, I’m John.

I’m a business graduate from Wilfrid Laurier University, I work in finance in Toronto, and I’ve been tutoring high school math for two years. I finished high school with top marks in calculus, advanced functions and physics, kept that up through university, and I haven’t forgotten which parts of those courses felt impossible the first time through.

That’s where my sessions focus. Before we start a unit, I work through it myself the way it’s likely to be tested. Then we go step by step: why a method works, not just which formula to memorize, followed by practice on the kinds of questions that actually show up on tests. The goal is a student who can look at a problem they haven’t seen before and know how to start.

I’m patient, nobody gets made to feel slow for asking a question twice, and parents hear from me about how things are going.
